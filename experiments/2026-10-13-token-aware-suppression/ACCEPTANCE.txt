TOKEN-AWARE TEMPORARY SUPPRESSION — CANDIDATE ONLY

Match whole Unicode words (letters, numbers, underscore are word characters).
Target ASCII case-insensitive h followed by 2 through 16 m characters, and
whole let / me / run or go separated by whitespace. Align detector with the
same command-separator rule. No ordinary component-word bans.

Trigger remains repeated target concentration. Restriction lasts 60 completed
Unicode words within reasoning. Native speculative rows must account for
accepted output and tentative draft prefixes without committing draft state.
Carry actual generated-prefix lexical/UTF-8 state through continuation.

Genuine EOS, reasoning-end and tool-control tokens remain allowed and disarm
suppression immediately. A pending target word directly before those special
control tokens is exempt: terminating reasoning is preferable to trapping the
model or forcing it to extend the word. Ordinary punctuation/whitespace are
not exempt. Textual lookalikes must not silently act as genuine control tokens.

Independent oracle must verify visible output in known protected token spans.
Required cases: Hmm with fused punctuation/newlines, mixed case, supported m
counts, alternate tokenizations, split UTF-8, activation-prefix carryover,
word expiry, marker boundaries inside speculative blocks, request slot reuse,
concurrent unaffected requests, cancellation and image/prompt preservation.
Do not certify suppression solely from activation logs or successful arithmetic.

Stage Ring 1 only after offline and native targeted gates pass. Drain and wait
for idle before coordinated restart. Verify real gateway dispatch and preserve
all unrelated settings. Observe one full hour after passing canary and admission;
any attributable failure requires repair and restarts the clock. Ring 2 stays
unchanged until correctness and stability gates pass. Keep rollback published.

Only synthetic fixtures and aggregate evidence may enter Git. Private session
text, raw logs, request IDs, credentials and screenshots remain outside Git.
